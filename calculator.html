            Calculation methodology embedded in this file
            

              

                
Wind-stress adjustment

                
The standard 10 m wind speed is converted to the adjusted wind speed used by the SMB curves.

                Ua = 0.71 · U101.23
              

              

                
Unified wave growth

                
Dimensionless fetch and depth are evaluated with the Hurdle–Stive hyperbolic-tangent formulation for Hs and Ts.

                F̂ = gF/Ua²  ·  d̂ = gd/Ua²
              

              

                
Duration limitation

                
A finite storm duration is converted into an equivalent fetch, then passed through the same wave-growth equations.

                F̂eff = [(gt/Ua) / 65.9]3/2
              

              

                
Dispersion and stability

                
Finite-depth wavelength is solved by Newton–Raphson. The calculated H/L is checked against the Miche limit.

                L = gT²/(2π) · tanh(2πd/L)  ·  H/L ≤ 0.142 tanh(kd)
